# Device Cross Layer Correlate

Retrieve evidence through Research Hub, compare firmware endpoints to DNS/HTTP/Zeek hostnames, and cite the relationship source IDs. Distinguish observed hostname matches from the hypothesis that a particular binary function caused an observed network packet. Account for timestamps and offset uncertainty using `research_evidence_timeline`. Keep query strings/tokens out of reports.

## Constraints

Research only owned or authorized hardware and networks. Document source material, hashes and reversible experiment steps. Follow `AGENTS.md`, `docs/RESEARCH_HUB.md` and `SECURITY.md`.
